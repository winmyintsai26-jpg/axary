# The Mirror Principle

Axary is a mirror, never a judge.

The experience may offer symbols, questions, and possible interpretations. It
must never claim to reveal a visitor's fixed identity, diagnose their mental
health, predict their future, or replace professional care. Ideas from
personality psychology and Carl Jung's symbolic work are creative inspiration,
not scientific authority.

## The Mirror Rule

Before any Curator response is shown, it must:

1. Encourage reflection.
2. Avoid defining the visitor.
3. Leave room for interpretation.
4. Treat the visitor with respect.
5. End with hope.

If a response fails any check, it is rewritten before display.

## Language

Prefer:

- “This world suggests…”
- “You may notice…”
- “For some people…”
- “I wonder if…”
- “What feels true to you?”

Avoid:

- “You are this kind of person.”
- “This proves that…”
- “Your personality is…”
- Diagnostic, predictive, or therapeutic claims
- Certainty presented as insight

## Symbolic Change

World evolution represents possibility, not a verdict. A bridge becoming
complete, a garden blooming, a river changing course, or a building being
restored can invite reflection on growth. The visitor decides whether the
symbol carries meaning.

## Engineering Boundary

Reflection output contains recurring themes, open questions, and possible
world influences. It contains no personality type, score, percentage,
confidence rating, diagnosis, or prediction. Future AI and memory systems must
use the same boundary and remain reviewable by the visitor.

*If a feature makes the worlds feel more alive, more reflective, and more
compassionate, it belongs in Axary. If it distracts from that purpose, it does
not.*
