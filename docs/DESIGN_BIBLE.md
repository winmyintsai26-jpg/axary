# Axary Design Bible

## Vision

Axary is a peaceful symbolic universe for self-reflection. It should feel less
like software and more like a place someone can visit: quiet enough to hear
their own thoughts, mysterious enough to return to, and warm enough to feel
safe within.

Success is not measured by how quickly a visitor understands every feature.
Success is the moment they pause, feel something, and want to stay.

## Philosophy

Most digital products are optimized to capture attention. Axary is designed to
care for it.

There is no feed to conquer, personality score to optimize, or system that
claims to define a person. Worlds are encountered through reflection. Symbols
create questions rather than diagnoses. Encouragement is a gift, not a score.

Axary is a digital place:

- People visit rather than use it.
- People travel rather than scroll.
- People reflect rather than perform.
- People explore meaning rather than receive labels.

## Core Values

### Peace

The experience protects quiet. Motion, sound, color, and language should lower
pressure rather than demand attention.

### Wonder

Discovery should feel meaningful. The universe reveals itself slowly and
preserves room for mystery.

### Meaningful Worlds

Every visible world represents an inner dimension with an intentional symbolic
role. Worlds are never generated as decoration. Their environment, light,
weather, sound, architecture, and vegetation must communicate one coherent
emotional idea.

### Inner Courage

Axary should help people approach thoughts they may normally avoid. Reflection
can be gentle without becoming shallow.

### Self-Trust

Axary offers interpretations, never verdicts. A visitor remains the authority
on their own life.

### Care

Interactions should leave both the traveler and the artist feeling more human.

## Design Principles

1. **Atmosphere before interface.** The world is always primary; controls are
   quiet and appear only when needed.
2. **One meaningful choice at a time.** Avoid dense navigation and simultaneous
   calls to action.
3. **Let moments breathe.** Use generous space, patient timing, and deliberate
   transitions.
4. **Reveal, do not announce.** Prefer environmental cues and gentle discovery
   over tutorials, banners, and marketing language.
5. **Warmth within darkness.** Midnight creates calm; moonlight provides
   clarity; lantern light signals welcome.
6. **Motion must have emotional purpose.** Movement should suggest breath,
   distance, wind, orbit, or light—not decoration.
7. **Accessibility is part of the atmosphere.** Reduced motion, keyboard
   access, readable contrast, and responsive composition are foundational.
8. **Protect performance.** Wonder disappears when motion stutters.
9. **Every world has meaning.** Heart, Growth, Bonds, Purpose, and Soul are
   symbolic lenses, not decorative categories or scores.
10. **Discovery precedes arrival.** A distant world should first be understood
    through color, silhouette, atmosphere, and orbit. Entering it is a later,
    intentional threshold—not the automatic result of curiosity.

## Symbolic World Language

The first five worlds form the Book of Worlds. Heart is tender and rose-lit;
Growth is green and renewing; Bonds is amber and connected; Purpose is elevated
and directional; Soul is moonlit and inward.

Future personalized forms may be radically different, but each needs one
coherent emotional idea, a memorable silhouette, restrained detail, and enough
negative space to breathe. Personalization should make a world feel understood,
never procedurally decorated.

## Community Principles

- Reflection is not scored.
- Guidance is warm, clear, and non-judgmental.
- Personality language must never pretend to be diagnosis or treatment.
- Visitors retain authority over their own meaning.
- Quiet participation is valid; no one is pressured to disclose or continue.
- Safety and emotional care take priority over engagement.

## Things Axary Will Never Become

- An infinite feed
- A popularity contest
- A follower-count economy
- A marketplace disguised as a community
- A dashboard full of engagement metrics
- An attention-extraction machine
- A place where AI replaces human imagination
- A noisy collection of badges, streaks, alerts, and urgency
- A generic gallery grid
- A product that values growth above care
- A decorative galaxy filled with stars that belong to no one
- A personality test that reduces people to labels
- A replacement for professional mental-health care
- An AI authority that tells visitors who they are
