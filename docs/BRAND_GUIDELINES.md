# Axary Brand Guidelines

## Brand Idea

Axary is a quiet book of inner worlds.

The brand should feel timeless, literary, gentle, and slightly mysterious. It
must never feel like a technology company trying to look futuristic.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Deep Midnight | `#080C17` | Primary background |
| Depth | Night Blue | `#10182B` | Atmospheric layers and surfaces |
| Clarity | Moonlight | `#DFE7F3` | Primary text and quiet illumination |
| Welcome | Lantern Gold | `#E6BD7A` | Rare warmth, focus, and invitations |
| Distance | Quiet Blue | `#8F9DB6` | Secondary text and distant elements |
| Shadow | Near Black | `#02040B` | Vignettes and deep spatial contrast |

Lantern Gold is precious. Use it as a small sign of life within the night, not
as a general accent color.

## Typography

### Display and narrative

Use an elegant old-style serif with human proportions. Preferred direction:
Iowan Old Style, Baskerville, Palatino, or a carefully licensed equivalent.

- Regular weights
- Generous line height
- Restrained scale
- Sentence case for narrative language

### Interface

Use a quiet, highly legible humanist sans serif such as Avenir or a neutral
system fallback.

- Medium weight at most
- Slight tracking for short labels
- No oversized product-marketing typography

### Wordmark

AXARY is uppercase with wide tracking. It should appear as a distant name in
the sky, not a corporate logo stamped onto the scene.

## Spacing

Axary uses space as part of the composition.

- Base spacing unit: `4px`
- Common rhythm: `8`, `12`, `16`, `24`, `32`, `48`, `64`, `96`
- Important elements should have more space around them than conventional UI.
- Do not fill empty areas simply because they are available.
- On small screens, preserve emotional spacing before preserving decorative
  detail.

## Motion Principles

Motion should feel like breath, wind, orbit, drifting mist, or changing light.

- Default transitions: `600–1800ms`
- Ambient cycles: `8–20s`
- Prefer ease-out for arrivals and symmetrical easing for breathing motion.
- Parallax is subtle enough to be felt before it is noticed.
- Avoid bounce, elastic motion, rapid scaling, sharp flashes, and constant
  attention-seeking movement.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences without reducing visual meaning.

## Icon Philosophy

Icons should be rare.

When an icon is necessary, it should be:

- Thin but not fragile
- Geometric with softened corners
- Immediately understandable
- Free of decorative sci-fi language
- Shown with a text label when meaning could be ambiguous

Never build a gaming HUD or cover the world with floating interface symbols.

## Writing Tone

Axary speaks like The Curator: warm, brief, poetic, clear, and never
authoritative about another person's inner life.

### Write this way

- “Begin Your Journey”
- “Every person carries many worlds within them.”
- “This world is still becoming.”
- “There is no right way to begin.”

### Avoid

- Startup language: “unlock,” “supercharge,” “engage,” “go viral”
- Competitive language: “top creator,” “trending,” “best performing”
- Artificial urgency: “don’t miss out,” “act now”
- Technical language in the experience
- Poetry that obscures a necessary instruction
- Diagnostic claims or promises of treatment
- Language that tells a visitor what they must feel

The best Axary writing feels like an invitation, never a demand.
