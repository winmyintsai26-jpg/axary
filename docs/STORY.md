# The Story of Axary

Before there were worlds, there was only a patient dark.

It was not empty. It was listening.

Then, somewhere beyond distance, a small light appeared—not bright enough to
command the sky, only warm enough to invite another. That first light became
Axary.

## The Curator

No one remembers when the Curator arrived.

Some travelers describe a figure carrying a lantern between the stars. Others
say the Curator is simply the quiet feeling that leads you toward a world you
needed to find.

The Curator does not judge what belongs in the universe. The Curator keeps the
paths gentle, protects unfinished things, and reminds every artist that a small
light is still a light.

## Worlds

Every creator is given a world.

A world may begin as a bare horizon, a single room, a garden, a city, or a moon
with one warm window. It grows through the things its artist chooses to make
and the stories they choose to keep.

No world is ranked above another. A quiet world far from the traveled paths is
not less important. It is waiting for the right traveler.

## Artists

Artists are the keepers of worlds.

They do not need permission, mastery, or an audience to begin. A sketch can
become weather. A song can become an ocean. A sentence can become a road
through the mountains.

In Axary, creating is not a performance. It is a way of placing another light
in the dark.

## Travelers

Travelers move through Axary without a feed to hurry them.

They follow distant lights, quiet sounds, half-seen paths, and curiosity.
Sometimes they discover a world that feels as though it has been waiting only
for them.

They may leave encouragement behind—not as a number beneath the work, but as a
small lantern for the artist who made it.

And when the traveler is ready, they may return home and begin a world of their
own.
